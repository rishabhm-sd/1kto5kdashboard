Each row is one HIT-month cohort. Mt cells are new churns confirmed in that specific relative month (not cumulative) — e.g. Feb–26's row reads total sellers, then how many of them churned at M0, how many more at M1, and so on. A dash means that cohort hasn't lived long enough yet for that month to be observable.

Source: csv_upload.hit_master_data (team = HITS or hit2 = 1, excluding flagged good sellers) joined to weekly ad spend from nushop.gc_view_3 via Metabase — HITSChurn-GCView3, card 14636, for HITS; eligible_sellers (team is null and hit2 is null) via Churn Cohort Matrix Revenue, question 14577, for Revenue — both on dashboard 928 (Asia/Kolkata calendar), same churn rule and the same relative-month churn counts underlying the companion retention curve's Overall lines. Mt share = churns whose 3-week slump started in relative month t, summed across every cohort, ÷ total churns observed anywhere in that team's matrix (96 of 417 for HITS, 228 of 717 for Revenue) — so each team's seven points add up to 100%, and the denominator is churned sellers, not the whole tracked population. Only cohorts old enough to have lived through month t contribute to that point; hover any point for how many cohorts and sellers back it.
