Retention % = 1 − (cumulative sellers whose terminal 3-week slump has started by that month ÷ cohort size); the projected point for the running month uses the same rule against only the fully-elapsed weeks posted so far. The two bold "Overall" lines recompute their base at every point to just the sellers whose cohort is old enough to have reached that relative month (343→15 for HITS M0→M6, 635→110 for Revenue, currently), rather than holding the denominator fixed at the full tracked population — so a point's "n" shrinks as the line moves right, and the percentage reflects only cohorts that have actually lived that long. The axis now runs through M12; months beyond M6 are blank until a cohort actually ages into them.
