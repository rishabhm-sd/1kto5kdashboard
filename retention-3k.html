The sellers and cohorts behind the bold "Overall" line above. Each week's base is only the sellers whose HIT-month cohort has actually reached that relative week — not a fixed 417 held constant the whole way, so the base shrinks as the line moves right and only the older cohorts are left contributing.

Source: csv_upload.hit_master_data (team = HITS or hit2 = 1, excluding flagged good sellers — the same 417-seller population as the churn-cohort tab) joined to weekly marketing spend from nushop.gc_view_3 via Metabase, database 23 (Asia/Kolkata calendar). Cohort = the seller's HIT month; week 0 is the first ISO week (Mon–Sun) starting on or after the 1st of that month, so every seller in a monthly cohort shares the same week-0 anchor. Retained = that week's marketing spend ₹3,000 or more, a point-in-time test — a seller can drop out one week and come back the next, unlike the 3-consecutive-week churn rule used elsewhere. Only fully-completed ISO weeks are included; the in-progress week is dropped to avoid a false reporting-lag dip. The bold "Overall" line recomputes its base at every point to just the sellers whose cohort has reached that relative week (417→15 from W0 to W34), rather than holding the denominator fixed.
